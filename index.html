Conference

Frontiers in Supersymmetric Theories

11 – 15 January 2027 Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences (SIMIS)
18F Auditorium
Shanghai, China

Supersymmetry provides a remarkably powerful framework for exploring fundamental questions in quantum field theory, quantum gravity, and mathematical physics.

This conference brings together experts working on complementary aspects of supersymmetric field theory and supergravity, with particular emphasis on structures shared across different dimensions and on how exact results can illuminate more general problems in quantum field theory and quantum gravity.

Topics

  • Supersymmetric gauge theory and localization
  • Superconformal field theory, Higgs branch, and vertex operator algebra
  • Supergravity and precision holography
  • Superstring theory and M-theory

What is covered

There is no registration fee. Coffee breaks, lunches and the conference dinner on Tuesday are provided. Accommodation and travel are at participants' own expense; no financial support is available.

Visas

Many nationalities can now enter China visa-free for short stays; check the current policy for your passport. If you need an invitation letter for a visa application, request one on the registration form and we will issue it within a week.

Accommodation

Several hotels are within a ten-minute walk of the institute; see the Location page. Participants book their own rooms.

Payments

Almost everything in Shanghai, from taxis and the metro to restaurants and convenience stores, is paid with Alipay or WeChat Pay, which is more convenient than cash or card. Both apps accept an international Visa or Mastercard: install the app before you travel, add your card under the payment settings, and you can scan and pay everywhere. Alipay is the smoother of the two for foreign cards. Vendors must accept cash, but many places will struggle to give change, and foreign bank cards work at ATMs but only rarely at the till.
